⚗ Methodology
DIGITAL-SIM architecture and computational pipeline
🏗
Four-Stage Architecture
  • 01

    Synthetic Population Generation

    Synthea v3.2 generates 10,000 patients per therapeutic area (50,000 total) using US Census demographics, comorbidity co-modeling, medication adherence, and SDOH variable generation. Outputs HL7 FHIR R4 format.

  • 02

    Generative Augmentation (GAN + VAE)

    CTGAN (300 epochs, 256×256 hidden dims) generates 5,000 additional patients per area. Beta-VAE (β=4, latent dim=32) oversamples rare phenotypes — e.g., NSCLC with autoimmune comorbidity. Total: 75,000 virtual patients.

  • 03

    Agent-Based Disease Progression (ABM)

    Mesa (Python) framework simulates weekly state transitions: enrolled → responding / not responding → adverse event / dropout. 9–15 parameters per therapeutic area, calibrated via Metropolis-Hastings MCMC (10,000 draws).

  • 04

    Calibration & Outcome Projection

    1,296 protocol variants per therapeutic area × 100 bootstrap iterations. Calibrated against 18 historical trials (Informa Trialtrove 2001–2023). KL-divergence minimization for outcome distribution matching.

📄 Research Paper
Digital Twin–Enabled In-Silico Clinical Trial Simulation
Authors Janipelli Bhargav Ram, Mathukumalli Sai Raviteja
Affiliation IIHMR University (MBA Pharmaceutical Management)

A staggering 70% of compounds entering clinical trials fail in Phase II, costing $800M–$1.4B per failed program. DIGITAL-SIM is a unified computational pipeline that employs synthetic patient populations created by Synthea, generative adversarial networks, variational autoencoders, and agent-based modelling to simulate Phase II protocols before enrolling a single patient.

Validated against 18 historical Phase II trials with 78.4% directional accuracy (r=0.81), the framework projects a +20.1 percentage point improvement in Phase II success rates through virtual protocol optimization — a potential 61% relative uplift in industry-wide success rates.

⚠️
Limitations & Future Work
  • ⚠

    Calibration Circularity

    ABM parameters estimated using same dataset used for validation. Out-of-sample validation (n=6 trials) underway, results expected 2027.

  • ⚠

    US-Centric Demographics

    Synthea trained on US Census priors; limited applicability to global trial populations. Non-US extension modules in development.

  • ⚠

    Regulatory Acceptance

    In-silico data supplements, not substitutes, human trial results in FDA/EMA filings. New agency guidance is moving toward simulation-augmented packages.
